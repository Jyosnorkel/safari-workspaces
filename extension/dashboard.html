Before you save and close

Only URLs and titles are saved. Unsaved forms, history, scroll position, and page state cannot be restored. Save important work first. Restore opens URLs in their saved order.

AI connection

No model is connected and no browsing data is sent anywhere. When connected, suggestions will require your review. The planned input is tab titles and URLs with query strings and fragments removed; page text is excluded from this build.
